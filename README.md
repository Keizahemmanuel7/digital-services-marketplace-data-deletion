DATA DELETION REQUEST – DIGITAL SERVICES MARKETPLACE APP

This page explains how users of the Digital Services Marketplace App can request deletion of their account and personal data.

──────────────────────────────

YOUR DATA DELETION OPTIONS

Users may request either of the following:

1. ACCOUNT DELETION

You may request deletion of your Digital Services Marketplace App account and all associated personal data. Once your request is processed, your account and related information will be permanently removed, except where retention is required by law or legitimate business obligations.

2. SPECIFIC DATA DELETION

You may request deletion of certain personal data collected through the app without deleting your entire account. This allows you to continue using the app while removing selected information.

──────────────────────────────

HOW TO REQUEST DATA DELETION

To submit a data deletion request:

1. Send an email to: keizahemmanuel4@gmail.com

2. Use one of the following subject lines:
   
   - "Account Deletion Request – Digital Services Marketplace App"
   
   - "Data Deletion Request – Digital Services Marketplace App"

3. Include your account email address and details of the information you would like deleted.

──────────────────────────────

DATA THAT CAN BE DELETED

Depending on your request, we may delete:

- User account information
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number (if provided)
- Saved preferences
- Service request history
- Transaction history (where applicable)
- Uploaded documents, images, or media (where applicable)
- App usage information associated with your account
- Communication records submitted through the app

──────────────────────────────

ACCOUNT DELETION

If you request account deletion, we will delete your account and associated personal data, except where retention is required for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or financial record-keeping purposes.

──────────────────────────────

DATA RETENTION

Certain information may be retained for a limited period if required for:

- Legal obligations
- Fraud prevention
- Security investigations
- Dispute resolution
- Financial or transaction record keeping

After the applicable retention period expires, retained data will be securely deleted.

──────────────────────────────

PROCESSING TIME

We aim to process deletion requests within 7–14 business days, subject to legal and operational requirements.
